L&D toolkit

From request to results

Four tools, one cycle. Diagnose the need, plan the delivery, bring people with you, prove the value. Each step hands its findings to the next, so you only type things once.

1

LNA Builder

Find out whether training is the answer before you build anything.

You put in
The request, evidence at three levels, a Mager and Pipe screen, Gilbert causes, ranked needs.
You get
A diagnosis, priorities, interventions, a sponsor script and a one-page LNA.
Passes on
Gap, causes, evidence, audience and learning needs to step 2.
Open →
2

Delivery Planner

Turn the need into a blend that fits and a plan that sticks.

You put in
Objectives and the seven drivers: audience, budget, time, location, skill level, priority.
You get
The blend with reasons, the models behind it, a timeline, measures and a transfer plan.
Passes on
Project details to step 3. Learners, learner time and the blend to step 4.
Open →
3

Stakeholder Map

Bring the people who can make or break it with you.

You put in
Each stakeholder’s power, interest, influence and attitude.
You get
A power-interest map, an engagement plan, a contact calendar and a RACI.
Takes from
Programme, sponsor, start date and goal from step 2.
Open →
4

ROI Builder

Prove the value in the language finance uses.

You put in
Fully loaded costs, the benefit, attribution and confidence, the period.
You get
ROI, benefit-cost ratio, payback, NPV, a risk range and a business case.
Takes from
Learners, learner time and the blend from step 2.
Open →

How it flows. Each tool opens with a worked example from the CIPD Level 3 and 5 booklet. Clear it and start your own. At the bottom of each tool, send your findings on to the next step. Your work saves in this browser only.

Next: plan the delivery. Your gap, causes, evidence, audience and learning needs carry across to the Delivery Planner.

Next: bring people with you and prove the value. Send the programme to the Stakeholder Map, and the learners, learner time and blend to the ROI Builder.

Next: build the financial case. If you sent the plan from step 2, the ROI Builder already has your learners and learner time.

Open the ROI Builder →

That is the full cycle. When your results check comes in, go back to the LNA and test whether the gap has closed.
